Regional Command and Control Centre

The proposed Regional Command and Control Centre in Nairobi will serve as the operational hub for the DPI, supporting information integration, monitoring, analysis, training and coordination among participating countries and institutions.

Monitoring & Decision Support

The Centre will support the integration and interpretation of information required for monitoring human–nature and environmental risks and for informing regional decision-making.

Data & Digital Infrastructure

The Centre will connect cloud infrastructure, geospatial information, Earth observation, ground observations and institutional datasets within the broader DPI environment.

Training & Capacity Development

Regional capacity development will enable participating institutions to use the infrastructure effectively, interpret information and apply digital tools to practical environmental and human-security challenges.

Transboundary Ecosystems

Many of Africa's most important ecosystems extend across national borders. The project therefore focuses on transboundary landscapes where wildlife movements, environmental change and human–nature interactions require cooperation between neighbouring countries.

Tsavo–Mkomazi

A Kenya–Tanzania landscape requiring coordinated approaches to wildlife, ecosystems and human–nature interactions.

Amboseli–Kilimanjaro

A transboundary ecosystem where wildlife movement and human activities extend across the Kenya–Tanzania border.

Mara–Serengeti

One of East Africa's most important connected ecosystems, requiring information and cooperation across national boundaries.

Lake Victoria

A shared ecosystem involving Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, where environmental and resource-related challenges require regional coordination.

Governance & Partnerships

The Regional DPI is envisaged as a collaborative initiative involving African governments, regional organisations, United Nations entities, research institutions and technology partners. The governance approach is intended to ensure that technology, data and institutional responsibilities develop together.

Nairobi–Valencia–Vienna Collaboration

The collaboration model links regional operations in Nairobi with United Nations and international capabilities in Vienna and scientific, technological and analytical expertise associated with Valencia.
