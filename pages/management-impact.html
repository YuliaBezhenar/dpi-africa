Project management

The project management framework brings together infrastructure, data, technology, governance, research and capacity development under a coordinated implementation structure. This approach is intended to ensure that the Regional DPI develops as an integrated regional public-interest initiative.

Implementation Components

Regional Command and Control Centre

Led through the Government of Kenya and the Lusaka Agreement Task Force, the Centre will provide the principal regional operational hub.

Cloud Infrastructure & UN Maps

The United Nations Global Service Centre will contribute to the data-centre, cloud infrastructure and UN Maps interface.

Geospatial Data

The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs will support access to relevant geospatial and Earth observation information.

Ground Data

Participating countries will contribute relevant ground information through designated national focal points.

Analytics & AI

UNGSC, UNOOSA and RESTEC will contribute to data analytics and AI integration, with academic collaboration welcomed.

DPI Governance & System Development

LATF, UNGSC, UNOOSA and RESTEC will contribute to the development of the governance framework and system architecture.

Training & Capacity Development

Regional partners will support training and institutional capacity development to ensure sustained use of the DPI.

Expected impact

The Regional DPI is designed to generate benefits across four interconnected dimensions: people, nature, institutions and resilience. It seeks to support safer communities and livelihoods, strengthen biodiversity and wildlife protection, improve institutional coordination and enhance preparedness for interconnected environmental and human–nature risks.

Implementation & Sustainability

The proposed implementation framework moves from institutional mobilisation and DPI development towards monitoring, research, capacity development, consolidation and regional scaling. Long-term sustainability will depend on institutional ownership, regional cooperation, technical capacity and the continued development of shared Digital Public Goods.
