From Data to Regional Intelligence

The Regional DPI will provide a common digital foundation for bringing together diverse sources of information and transforming them into actionable intelligence for disaster risk reduction and human–nature security.

Earth Observation & Geospatial Information

Satellite and geospatial information provide essential evidence for monitoring environmental change, ecosystems, land use, climate impacts and emerging risks across national boundaries.

Ground & Institutional Data

Ground observations and institutional datasets complement Earth observation by providing information on conditions, events and impacts that cannot be understood from satellite data alone.

Data Integration & Interoperability

The DPI will connect these different information sources through interoperable systems, enabling participating institutions and countries to share information and develop a more comprehensive regional understanding of risk.

Analytics & AI

Integrated data can support advanced analytics and AI-enabled approaches for identifying patterns, assessing risks, monitoring changes and supporting decision-makers.

Early Warning & Decision Support

The ultimate purpose is to transform information into timely decision support, supporting risk assessment, early warning, monitoring, coordinated response and longer-term resilience.

Project Objectives

The project brings together capacity development, transboundary collaboration, research, governance and sustainable institutional ownership to ensure that the DPI becomes a functioning regional public-interest infrastructure rather than simply a technology platform.

Technical Work Packages

The project will address seven complementary areas:

genetic tracking and forensic tracing

agro-pastoral conflict analysis

human-wildlife conflict and poaching

Digital Public Goods and remote sensing/GIS

economic and social policy analysis

water resources and disaster-risk reduction

climate-change assessment
